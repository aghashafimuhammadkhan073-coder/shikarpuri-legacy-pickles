About Shikarpuri Legacy Pickles

Traditional taste since day one. Real taste, real achar.

Who we are

Shikarpuri Legacy Pickles is a Pakistani pickle brand offering Shikarpuri-style achar, chutneys and murabbas. Our slogans say it best: Traditional Taste Since Day One, Real Taste · Real Achar and Famous Shikarpuri Pickles.

What we offer

Our menu has 37 varieties in five categories:

  • Oil Pickles – 15 varieties in 400g and 800g packs
  • Vinegar Pickles – 7 varieties in 400g and 800g packs
  • Export Quality Pickles – 5 varieties in 400g and 800g packs
  • Chutneys – 5 varieties in 1kg packs
  • Murabbas – 5 varieties in 1kg packs

Shikarpur and its achar

Shikarpur is a city in Sindh, Pakistan, widely known for its pickles. Read What is Shikarpuri achar? to learn more.
