About Us

Real Taste, Real Achar

Shikarpuri Legacy Pickles brings you a wide range of achar, chutneys and murabbas in the traditional Shikarpuri style — from everyday favourites like Mix and Mango pickle to special varieties such as Lasooda, Bheh, Murgh and Qurma.

  • Oil pickles, vinegar pickles and an Export Quality range
  • Pickles in 400g and 800g jars
  • Chutneys and murabbas in 1kg packs
  • Quick ordering and enquiries on WhatsApp
Simple & Quick

How to Order

  1. Choose

    Browse the menu and pick your favourite achar, chutney or murabba.

  2. Select pack size

    Pickles come in 400g or 800g. Chutneys and murabbas are 1kg.

  3. Tap WhatsApp

    The product name is filled in for you — just send the message.

  4. Confirm

    We reply with price and availability, and you confirm your order.
